# P2 v2 results: Collars Under the Microscope, buy-side upgrade

Generated by `scripts/v2/make_results_v2.py` from `results/v2/tables/*`. Pre-registration v2 hash `11f656cd0a6f` (frozen before any v2 test). v1 results are unchanged. Reset-day timing luck in costless put-spread collars is already published (Braun, Hoffstein, Israelov, Nze Ndong 2023, see `evidence_v2.md` S1); this is a free-data replication with extensions.

**Timing.** Strikes, fills and every signal are set at the 16:00 close of the reset day from data up to that close and held to the next reset. **Costs.** v1 half-spread paid on every leg at each reset; no commissions, no market impact; capacity is not modelled (descriptive scale check only). Sharpe ratios carry 95% stationary-bootstrap CIs and day counts. Every table that includes dates after 2019 is labelled `seen period (v1 reported it)`. Research, not investment advice.
